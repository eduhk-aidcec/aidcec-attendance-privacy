App and developer

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and related data for AIDCEC Attendance, the mobile app of the AIEP Teaching and Learning Platform, used for the MSc(AIEP) programme and related courses at The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK). The platform was previously branded AIDCEC; the mobile apps are still listed under that name on the App Store and Google Play.

Important: SSO login

Students and staff sign in with EdUHK Single Sign-On (SSO). Your university login is managed by EdUHK, not by this app. Requesting deletion below removes your platform account and app-related data we hold — it does not delete your EdUHK SSO or Microsoft 365 account. For university login account requests, contact EdUHK IT.

How to request account deletion

There is no in-app self-service delete button. To request deletion:

  1. Email the MSc(AIEP) Programme Office at aiep@eduhk.hk (Tel: 2948 7275; programme page www.aidcec.eduhk.hk/msc-aiep).
  2. State that you are requesting account and related data deletion for AIDCEC Attendance / AIEP Teaching and Learning Platform.
  3. Include your full name, EdUHK email address, and student or staff number so we can verify your identity.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (generally within 40 days).

What data is deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete or permanently de-identify, where applicable:

  • Your platform account profile (name, email, student/staff number, role linkages in our database).
  • App session tokens and device login session data we control.
  • Peer-assessment, team, or presentation-practice content tied solely to your account, where retention is not required.

What may be retained (and for how long)

  • Attendance and academic records may be retained for the academic period and as long as required for course administration, university record-keeping, or legal obligations. Where full deletion is not permitted, we will explain what must be retained and for how long.
  • Presentation-practice recordings (if you used that feature) are retained for a limited period (currently up to two years) unless deleted earlier where policy allows.
  • Backup copies may remain for a short operational period until rotated out of backup systems, then are permanently removed.

Related policy

Full details are in our Privacy Policy (sections on retention and your rights).

繁體中文

應用程式及營運者

本頁說明如何要求刪除 AIDCEC Attendance 的帳戶及相關資料。該應用程式屬 AIEP 教學平台，用於香港教育大學高級管理專業人員人工智能理學碩士（MSc(AIEP)）課程及相關科目。 本平台前稱 AIDCEC；手機應用程式在 App Store 及 Google Play 上仍以該名稱上架。

重要提示：SSO 登入

學生及教職員使用教大單一登入（SSO）。你的大學登入帳戶由教大管理，並非由本應用程式管理。 以下刪除要求只涉及我們所持有的平台帳戶及應用程式相關資料，不會刪除你的教大 SSO 或 Microsoft 365 帳戶。大學登入帳戶事宜請聯絡教大資訊科技服務處。

如何要求刪除帳戶

應用程式內並無自助刪除按鈕。請按下列步驟提出要求：

  1. 電郵至 MSc(AIEP) 課程辦事處 aiep@eduhk.hk（電話：2948 7275；課程網頁 www.aidcec.eduhk.hk/msc-aiep）。
  2. 明確說明你要求刪除 AIDCEC Attendance／AIEP 教學平台的帳戶及相關資料。
  3. 提供全名、教大電郵及學號或職員編號，以便核實身份。

我們將於《個人資料（私隱）條例》所要求的時限內（一般為40 日內）回覆可核實的請求。

將會刪除的資料

  • 平台帳戶資料（姓名、電郵、學號／職員編號、角色關聯等我們資料庫所存內容）。
  • 我們所控制的應用程式登入權杖及裝置工作階段資料。
  • 僅與你帳戶相關、且無法定保留必要的朋輩評估／分組／匯報練習內容。

可能保留的資料（及期限）

  • 出席及學術紀錄可能因課程行政、大學存檔或法律義務而須保留至相關學年／政策所定期間。 若未能完全刪除，我們會說明須保留的內容及期限。
  • 匯報練習錄影（如有使用）目前最長保留約兩年；若政策允許並依你的要求處理，可提前刪除。
  • 備份副本可能短暫保留至輪替後永久清除。
